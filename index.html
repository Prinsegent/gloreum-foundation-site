Foundation

A phased ecosystem under responsible development

Gloreum is being developed through a phased foundation process: legal and governance setup, identity and Executive onboarding, Acts and PPV pilot, Energy and Crystal Mine pilot, Gloret and Forge testing, and controlled GEC utility.

Human Proof Mining

Mining begins with validated participation, not empty tapping or hardware competition.

Ethereum-Derived Network

Gloreum Network is planned as an Ethereum-compatible blockchain using GLO as gas.

Enterprise Layer

G-COREs create productive demand, work opportunities, and mission-aligned enterprise activity.

Development Status

Minimum Viable Ecosystem first

The first build objective is the Minimum Viable Ecosystem: Executive onboarding, Acts, Proof of Participation Validation, Energy, Crystal Mine, Gloret records, and Forge simulation.
