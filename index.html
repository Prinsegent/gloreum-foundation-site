Ecosystem

The Gloreum Human Participation Ecosystem

Gloreum begins with the human being. It structures contribution through Acts, protects truth through Proof of Participation Validation, activates mining through Energy, forms assets through the Forge and Lab, circulates value through GEC, and creates enterprise through G-COREs.

Acts

Structured participation through Logos, Ethos, Kratos, and Poros.

PPV

Proof of Participation Validation protects participation truth and eligibility.

Energy

Non-transferable mining authorization earned through validated Acts.

Glorets

Raw participation-mined formation assets produced through Human Proof Mining.

GEC

The primary formed ecosystem coin created through the Forge.

G-COREs

Human-led, AI-coordinated, blockchain-based enterprise systems.

Blockchain

Gloreum Network

Gloreum Network is planned as an Ethereum-derived blockchain infrastructure. GLO powers transactions and smart contract execution as the native gas coin. GEC remains the formed ecosystem coin created through Gloreum formation processes.
