Gloreum Implementation Roadmap

Gloreum is being built through a controlled implementation strategy: build the foundation first, test each layer, then scale only what is ready.

Phase 1Foundation, legal structuring, IP, governance charter, and risk framework.
Phase 2Identity, Executive onboarding, Voice Signature, and registration Crystal allocation.
Phase 3Acts and PPV pilot.
Phase 4Energy and Crystal Mine pilot.
Phase 5Gloret and Forge testing.
Phase 6GEC internal utility.
Phase 7Social Circles and Acts expansion.
Phase 8Marketplace pilot.
Phase 9Jive, Mirror Pay, and Glass Account pilot.
Phase 10GALORE and G-CORE pilot.
Phase 11Treasury and impact financing pilot.
Phase 12–14Governance expansion, external partnerships, liquidity review, scale, and optimization.
