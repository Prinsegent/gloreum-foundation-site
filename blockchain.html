Blockchain

Gloreum Network

Gloreum Network is planned as an Ethereum-derived blockchain infrastructure. GLO is the native gas coin for transaction fees and smart contract execution. GEC remains the formed ecosystem coin created through the Forge.

GLO / GEC Distinction

GLO powers the Gloreum blockchain.

GEC is formed through ecosystem participation and asset formation processes.

Planned Network Phases

1. DevnetPrivate internal Ethereum-derived Gloreum development network.
2. Internal TestnetControlled validator and smart contract testing.
3. Public TestnetLimited community testing, faucet, explorer, and app integration.
4. Beta NetworkGoverned pre-mainnet network with deeper security review.
5. MainnetPublic network launch subject to technical, legal, security, and governance approval.
