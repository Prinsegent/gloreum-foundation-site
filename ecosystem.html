Ecosystem

The Gloreum Human Participation Ecosystem

Gloreum moves from identity and participation into validation, Energy, mining, formation, enterprise, settlement, treasury stewardship, governance, and development.

Executives

Registered participants who complete Acts, earn Energy, mine Glorets, build reputation, and access opportunity pathways.

Acts

Structured participation through Logos, Ethos, Kratos, and Poros.

PPV

Proof of Participation Validation confirms contribution and establishes Participation Truth.

Energy

Non-transferable mining authorization earned through validated Acts.

Crystal Mine

The portal where Executives view Crystals, Energy balances, rotation, Gloret output, Boosts, and records.

Glorets

Raw participation-mined formation assets produced by the Crystals.

Forge

The formation system that transforms eligible Glorets and inputs into GEC.

G-COREs

Human-led, AI-coordinated, blockchain-based enterprises aligned with Gloreum sectors.

Social Circles

Governed human coordination structures for participation, work, exchange, support, and mission execution.
